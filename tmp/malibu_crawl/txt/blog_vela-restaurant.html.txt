The Malibu Hotel
Vella Restaurant
One morning waking up in Malibu, listening to melodious music, sipping a cup of coffee and having breakfast in Vela restaurant on 3rd floor with more than 40 various buffet dishes from Asia to Europe before starting your new day of enthusiasm for a business trip in Vung Tau or a fun day out in the beautiful coastal city.
The hotel culinary team knowledgeable the quintessence in cuisine will take care of every dish from choosing fresh ingredients to processing for an energetic breakfast, or a light lunch, to even dishes for a special dinner for an impressive Gala night.
The restaurant is designed with spacious modern styling and high-quality carpet. It accommodates up to 350 guests and serves à la carte, buffet and Gala
