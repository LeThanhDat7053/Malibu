Our Malibu Hotel Destination For Your Special Day
Whether you’re planning an intimate gathering or a large celebration, our team of professionals will help you design your dream day at our Malibu wedding venues.
Our Vung Tau wedding packages anticipate your needs, including photography, flowers and more. Our range of Malibu wedding venues are flexible, and can host a reception from 50 to 250 guests. The Malibu Hotel meeting and event spaces (Bluefish 1 and 2, Yellowtail 1 and 2, Stingray, Malibu Ballroom 1 and 2) are accessible. For more information, please view our
Accessibility Statement.
Working with our professional catering team will allow you to create a menu that captures the essence of your special day.
Your wedding is just the beginning of your life together. Our Concierge staff will be happy to assist you in planning your Hawaii honeymoon. In addition, our Concierges can assist with island excursions for your wedding guests, showcasing the full beauty of your destination wedding.
